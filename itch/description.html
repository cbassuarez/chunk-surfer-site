THE NIGHT AHEAD

01 / 03

ONE LAST NIGHT AT ELLERY.

Demolition is scheduled for six. You’ve been hired to record five rooms at Ellery Conservatoire before then.

The pub is still serving. At the vigil, Ruth numbers the cups so she can tell who’s gone home.

A first-person horror adventure about a night’s work that takes you far beyond the job.

RECORDING

02 / 03

WHAT GOES ON THE TAPE.

A studio, a drained swimming pool, a concert hall, the practice wing and a chapel. Five clean room tones are on the contract. Recording them means more than standing still: tracing unwanted sounds, working the equipment by hand, and listening back to what you brought away.

IMPOSSIBLE SPACES

03 / 03

THE NIGHT OPENS OUT.

An instrument becomes a place you can walk through. A road carries you into a storm. A performance leaves room for a conversation. The night moves between ordinary work and places that do not fit the plan.

FIELD RECORDING

01 / 03

LISTENING IS PART OF THE WORK.

A clean take begins with the room itself. There are sound sources to find, equipment to handle and interruptions to deal with. You can listen back to your takes and see what gets marked on the work order. The world carries on while you work.

EXPLORATION

02 / 03

WHEN THE NIGHT TURNS HOSTILE.

Exploration and physical puzzles share the night with turn-based combat and timed defence. Your field equipment becomes a way to expose, monitor and answer a threat. Assistance settings can ease recording, navigation, combat and timed escapes.

MUSICAL ARCHITECTURE

03 / 03

THE INSTRUMENT IS THE ROOM.

Strings pass overhead. Casework becomes a route. You move through musical instruments at the scale of buildings, finding landings, doors and another way onward. You still have to find your footing.

ELLERY

01 / 04

WHO STILL NEEDS THIS PLACE?

Iona at the Lyric would like her bottle opener back. Outside the school, people are keeping vigil and disagreeing about its future. Students have demands of their own. An ordinary errand can bring you into an account of Ellery that the paperwork leaves out.

ARCHITECTURE

02 / 04

A GLASSHOUSE AFTER DARK.

Beyond the rooms on the work order, Ellery has corridors, gardens and places that have their own life after hours. Follow an ordinary passage and see where it leads.

PERFORMANCES

03 / 04

ROOM FOR A PERFORMANCE.

A performance can be company. It can also ask more of you than you expected. Sit beside another listener, talk about the work, and decide how much of the night to give it.

ONWARD AND RETURN

04 / 04

ANOTHER WAY INTO THE NIGHT.

The night has more than one ending. Recordings, encounters and filed returns give you more to find on another visit. Some completed runs open another way through the night. The archive keeps track of what you have discovered.

LOCAL PROFILE OPTIONAL

Microphone. Permission is optional. With permission, room loudness is measured locally. You can choose to record spoken slates and voicemail; those recordings stay on your device. There is no speech recognition or automatic audio upload.

Local profile. A separate optional local profile permits disclosed personalization using names, computer details, and in-game responses. The complete game remains available without microphone input or profiling.

Desktop effects. Optional effects can move the game window, open game-owned windows, and create files in the game’s local data folder. Disable modules, restore the window, or erase profile data in SETTINGS.

Controls and assistance. Use keyboard and mouse or a controller. Adjust text speed, flashes, screen shake, controller bindings, recording forgiveness, navigation help, combat assistance, and escape timers in Settings.

CONTENT WARNING

Sustained dread, sudden loud sounds, deliberate jumpscares, flashing lights, and high-contrast patterns. Reduce visual effects in Settings. Optional desktop effects may move the game window and open additional windows.
